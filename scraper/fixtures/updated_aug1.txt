TITLE: [UPDATED] Switching power interruptions on August 1 and 2 in Davao City and Davao del Norte
URL: https://www.davaolight.com/post/updated-switching-power-interruptions-on-august-1-and-2-in-davao-city-and-davao-del-norte
PUBLISHED: 2026-07-29T08:00:00.000Z

This scheduled power interruption advisory has been updated. The second set of switching power interruptions (indicated in gray text) has been cancelled. This decision follows the July 31 emergency power outage caused by a bumped pole incident.

Davao Light and Power Co., Inc. (Davao Light) will conduct switching power interruptions on Saturday and Sunday, August 1 and 2, 2026, affecting some parts of Davao City. The first power interruption is necessary to facilitate line works. This will be for 45 minutes at any time between 1:00 a.m. and 2:00 a.m. on Saturday, August 1.

Specifically affected are customers from Bangkal, Matina, Puan, and Dumoy.

Davao Light apologizes for the inconvenience caused by these scheduled power interruptions.
