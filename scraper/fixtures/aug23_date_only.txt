TITLE: Switching power interruptions on August 23 in Davao City, Panabo City, and Carmen
URL: https://www.davaolight.com/post/switching-power-interruptions-on-august-23-in-davao-city-panabo-city-and-carmen
PUBLISHED: 2026-08-21T08:00:00.000Z

Davao Light and Power Co., Inc. (Davao Light) will conduct switching power interruptions on Sunday, August 23, 2026, affecting some parts of Davao City, Panabo City, and the Municipality of Carmen in Davao del Norte. These are necessary to facilitate 69 kV DRA 3 line repair works.

Davao Light apologizes for the inconvenience caused by these scheduled power interruptions.
