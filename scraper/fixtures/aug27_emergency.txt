TITLE: Emergency power interruptions on August 27 to 31 in Talicud Island
URL: https://www.davaolight.com/post/emergency-power-interruptions-on-august-27-to-31-in-talicud-island
PUBLISHED: 2026-08-27T02:00:00.000Z

This emergency power interruption advisory has been updated. The power interruptions (indicated in gray text) initially scheduled for August 28, 29, 30, and 31 have been cancelled as NPC repairs for the Talicud Diesel Power Plant are now complete. [Updated on August 28 at 4:30 p.m.]

Davao Light and Power Co., Inc. (Davao Light) will conduct emergency power interruptions today, August 27, until Monday, August 31, 2026, affecting portions of Talicud Island due to generator trouble at the NPC - Talicud Diesel Power Plant.

These power interruptions will be for 4 hours starting at any time between 5:00 p.m. and 10:00 p.m.

Today, August 27, customers in Mansud and nearby areas will be affected by this emergency power outage.

Davao Light apologizes for the inconvenience caused by these emergency power interruptions.
