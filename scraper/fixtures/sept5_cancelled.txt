TITLE: CANCELLED: Switching power interruptions on September 5 and 6 in Davao City
URL: https://www.davaolight.com/post/switching-power-interruptions-on-september-5-and-6-in-davao-city
PUBLISHED: 2026-09-03T08:00:00.000Z

This scheduled power interruption has been cancelled due to necessary operational adjustments. We will share the new schedule as soon as it is available. [Updated on September 5 at 8:35 a.m.]

Davao Light and Power Co., Inc. (Davao Light) will conduct switching power interruptions on Saturday and Sunday, September 5 and 6, 2026, affecting some parts of Davao City. These are necessary to facilitate line works along MacArthur Highway.

To facilitate the necessary works, an 8-hour power interruption will be implemented from 11:00 p.m. on Saturday, September 5, to 7:00 a.m. on Sunday, September 6. Customers from Matina Baptist Church along the corner of MacArthur Highway and Quimpo Boulevard to Alexian Brothers and nearby areas will be affected by this service disruption.

Specifically affected are customers from Davao Light Substation along Km. 5 Guadalupe Village, Matina Pangi Road to MacArthur Highway, Matina, up to Matina Baptist Church, including NCCC Centerpoint and nearby areas.

Davao Light apologizes for the inconvenience caused by these scheduled power interruptions.
