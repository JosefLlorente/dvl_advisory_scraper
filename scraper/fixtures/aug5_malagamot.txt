TITLE: 6-hour power interruption on August 5 in Davao City
URL: https://www.davaolight.com/post/6-hour-power-interruption-on-august-5-in-davao-city
PUBLISHED: 2026-08-01T08:00:00.000Z

Davao Light and Power Co., Inc. (Davao Light) will conduct a six-hour power interruption from 8:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. on Wednesday, August 5, 2026, for pole replacement along Malagamot, Davao City.

Specifically affected are customers in Malagamot from Shell Gas Station to Cambridge Subdivision, including DCWD Panacan Water System and nearby areas.

Davao Light apologizes for the inconvenience caused by this scheduled power interruption.
