TITLE: Switching power interruptions on September 6 and 7 in the Cities of Davao and Panabo
URL: https://www.davaolight.com/post/switching-power-interruptions-on-september-6-and-7-in-the-cities-of-davao-and-panabo
PUBLISHED: 2026-09-04T08:00:00.000Z

Davao Light and Power Co., Inc. (Davao Light) will conduct switching power interruptions on Sunday and Monday, September 6 and 7, 2026, affecting some parts of Davao City and Panabo City.

The first switching power interruption is necessary to facilitate substation works at San Pedro Substation. This will be for 45 minutes at any time between 5:00 a.m. and 6:00 a.m. on Sunday, September 6, affecting customers in the Panabo Wharf area, from Petron Gas Station to Anflo Industrial Estate, Stanfilco Wharf, Panabo Coast Guard, to Ornels Sea Foods and nearby areas.

To facilitate the necessary works, an 8-hour power interruption will be implemented from 12:00 a.m. to 8:00 a.m. on Sunday, September 6, affecting customers from the crossing of Damosa to Jetti Gas, opposite SM Lanang, and nearby areas.

Specifically affected are customers from Bioessence to Circumferential Road, Bacaca, DMSF, Abreeza, and nearby areas; a portion of J. P. Laurel Avenue in Lanang, crossing Arroyo Street up to Shell opposite Davao Light Bajada Substation, and nearby areas; and a portion of J. P. Laurel Avenue in Lanang, crossing Arroyo Street up to DOH XI near SPMC, including SM Lanang, and nearby areas.

Davao Light apologizes for the inconvenience caused by these scheduled power interruptions.
