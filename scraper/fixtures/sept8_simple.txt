TITLE: Scheduled power interruption on September 8 in Davao City
URL: https://www.davaolight.com/post/scheduled-power-interruption-on-september-8-in-davao-city
PUBLISHED: 2026-09-05T06:06:35.920Z

Davao Light and Power Co., Inc. (Davao Light) will conduct a ten-hour switching power interruption from 5:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. on Tuesday, September 8, 2026, to facilitate net metering and recloser installation at Hedcor Talomo Plant 3.

Specifically affected are customers from Purok 24 Samulco Village along Catalunan Pequeno Road going to Cawa Cawa, including Hedcor Talomo Plant 3, Villa Constancia Subdivision, and nearby areas.

Davao Light apologizes for the inconvenience caused by this scheduled power interruption.
