TITLE: Switching power interruptions on July 18 and 19 in Davao City
URL: https://www.davaolight.com/post/switching-power-interruptions-on-july-18-and-19-in-davao-city
PUBLISHED: 2026-07-16T08:00:00.000Z

Davao Light and Power Co., Inc. (Davao Light) will conduct switching power interruptions on Saturday and Sunday, July 18 and 19, 2026, affecting some parts of Davao City.

The first power interruption is necessary to facilitate line works in the Indangan and Acacia areas. This will be an 8-hour power interruption from 10:00 p.m. on Saturday, July 18, to 6:00 a.m. on Sunday, July 19, affecting customers from Palos Verdes Indangan entrance to Acacia up to Purok San Roque, Acacia, and nearby areas.

Specifically affected are customers in Pampanga, from Insular Village Lanang to DPWH Panacan, including Sasa and nearby areas.

Davao Light apologizes for the inconvenience caused by these scheduled power interruptions.
